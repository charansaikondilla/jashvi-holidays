WHO WE ARE

Founded with a vision to make travel more accessible, enjoyable and seamless, Jashvi Holidays combines extensive industry knowledge with a fresh, customer-centric approach to travel planning and management.

As a new venture backed by 15 years of industry experience, we bring together established expertise and a forward-looking approach to build lasting relationships with customers, corporate clients and travel partners.
